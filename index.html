Methodology and calibration

Blend. First 48 hours: HRRR 35%, National Blend 25%, ECMWF 20%, GFS 10%, ICON and GEM 5% each. After 48 hours: ECMWF 35%, National Blend 30%, GFS 15%, ICON and GEM 10% each. Each model is sampled at the grid cell nearest the base, mid-mountain and summit, with temperatures adjusted to the true elevation.

Calibration to Breckenridge. Each model's past forecasts were compared with the daily snow and water measured by the National Weather Service co-op observer in Breckenridge (station USC00050909, read around 8 AM), November through April. Each model's water is scaled by the factor below before blending. The calibration refreshes monthly.

Elevation.

Snow ratio. Ratios fall quickly above freezing; some wet snow still accumulates up to about 40°F model temperature. A 15% reduction when summit gusts exceed 35 mph is an assumption and has not been tested.

Known limits. When the models forecast snow, corrected amounts are about right on average. Most large errors come from storms the models miss or mistime, so on big storm days this forecast still tends to run low. Treat the top of the range as a real possibility.

Live data: Open-Meteo (HRRR, NBM, ECMWF IFS, GFS, ICON, GEM, GEFS and ECMWF ensembles) and NWS Boulder gridpoint forecast, retrieved in your browser at load and every 30 minutes. Calibration: NOAA GHCN-Daily USC00050909 (Breckenridge), Open-Meteo historical forecasts, and Breck season totals as listed by OnTheSnow.
