How this forecast works

Three elevations. Each model is sampled at the grid cell nearest the base, mid-mountain and summit, with temperatures adjusted to the true elevation. Mid-mountain is calibrated to match Breck's own snow reports.

What history says about errors. When the models forecast snow, the corrected amounts were about right on average. The misses are mostly storms the models didn't see coming or mistimed, so on big storm days even the corrected forecast tends to run low. Treat the top of the range as a real possibility. Wind-direction adjustments were tested against SNOTEL data and did not help, so none are applied.

Live data: Open-Meteo (HRRR, NBM, ECMWF IFS, GFS, ICON, GEM, GEFS, ECMWF ENS) and the NWS Boulder gridpoint forecast, fetched in your browser each time the page loads. Calibration data: NOAA GHCN-Daily station USC00050909 (Breckenridge), Open-Meteo historical forecasts, NRCS SNOTEL, and Breck's season totals as listed by OnTheSnow.
